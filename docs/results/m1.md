Recorded 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10 on the author's machine (Intel Core i7-13700H, 13.7 GB RAM,
Windows 11, Python 3.14.5). Up to ten other builds ran on the machine at the same time, so the
executions-per-second figures are low and noisy; execution counts, edges and findings are
deterministic for a given RNG seed and do not depend on the machine. CI re-runs the suite on
Ubuntu with Python 3.12 and runs the Atheris job, which cannot run on Windows.

## Seeds and dictionaries (`make bench`)

`fuzzcamp bench --runs 10000 --seeds 0,1,2,3,4`: each target in three set-ups, five RNG seeds,
10,000 executions per run. `bare` starts from one empty input with no dictionary. Line coverage
counts lines inside the target file's functions.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| kvconfig | seeds | 96.0 | 97.6% | 0/5 | - |

| regex | bare | 146.6 | 88.3% | 0/5 | - |

The whole bench took 13 min 19 s on the loaded machine (213 to 5,610 executions per second
depending on the target). Raw rows: `.tmp/bench.json` (not committed; re-run `make bench`).

### What the numbers show (Analyze)

- Seeds add coverage on every target: +4.6 edges on kvconfig, +9.4 on regex, +8.2 on rules and
  +21 on canary, where bare runs never get past the 5-byte magic header.
- The dictionary adds coverage on regex (+2.0) and rules (+3.4) and is what finds the canary bug:
  the `CHK` keyword is only in the dictionary, and with it all five runs crash, after a median of
  901 executions; with seeds alone none do in 10,000.
- On kvconfig the dictionary does not help (95.6 against 96.0 edges): its seeds already contain
  every token, and dictionary mutations take a share of the budget from byte mutations. This is
  reported as measured, not hidden.
- None of the three real parsers produced a finding in any bench run.

## Cost of coverage tracing

Each packaged seed run 300 times with and without `sys.settrace` (seeds are full, valid inputs,
slower than the average mutated input): kvconfig 121 us against 2,525 us (20.8x), regex 649 us
against 6,641 us (10.2x), rules 234 us against 4,468 us (19.1x).

## Found and fixed during M1 development

- The first canary design (`CHK` taking a whole 1 to 3 digit argument) was never reached in 40,000
  executions even with the dictionary: every corpus entry containing `CHK` failed on the argument,
  and line edges give no gradient toward a digits-only argument. `CHK` now takes the first digit
  in its argument, and seeds+dict find the bug in 5 of 5 runs.
- `regex_parse` compiled its quantifier pattern on every call through `re.compile`; it is now a
  module constant. This also lets the differential-oracle test replace `re` in the module only.

## CI-only

- **Atheris** (`atheris==3.1.0`, Linux wheels only): 20 s per real parser, failing on any crash
  file, and up to 120 s on the canary, which must crash and replay through `fuzzcamp replay` to
  the expected `IndexError` key. Not run locally; results appear as `::notice` annotations.
- The suite on Python 3.12 and `pip-audit` in the `build` job, plus a canary bench in the job
  summary.
